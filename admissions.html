Admission Process

How to Apply

1

Step 1 - Enquiry

Visit school or fill online enquiry form to express your interest in joining our institution.

2

Step 2 - Application

Submit application form with required documents to the school office within the stipulated time.

3

Step 3 - Interaction

Student and parent interaction with school management to understand mutual expectations and goals.

4

Step 4 - Admission

Confirmation of admission and fee payment to secure the seat for the upcoming academic year.

5

Step 5 - Welcome

Orientation and welcome to the school family, introducing students and parents to the school culture.
