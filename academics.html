Curriculum

Our Academic Approach

We offer a well-rounded curriculum that aligns with the Tamil Nadu State Board while integrating essential Islamic teachings, ensuring students excel in both Deen and Dunya.

  • State Board Syllabus: Following the prescribed Tamil Nadu Matriculation Board curriculum.
  • Islamic Integration: Seamless integration of Islamic values across subjects.
  • Language Proficiency: Focus on English, Tamil, and Arabic fluency.
  • Modern IT Skills: Comprehensive computer education from an early age.
  • Global Readiness: Equipping students with skills for a rapidly changing world.
